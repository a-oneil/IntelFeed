Web pages

The features in this section work on the websites you visit without you opening the extension. They need permission to read all websites, which you grant once below. They send information only to your IntelFeed server, and they do not run on the IntelFeed website itself.

Permission needed

These features need permission to read all websites. Your browser will ask you to confirm.

Show a status dot on the IntelFeed toolbar button

The IntelFeed button is the IntelFeed icon in your browser's toolbar, next to the address bar. If you don't see it, open your browser's extensions menu (the puzzle-piece icon) and pin IntelFeed. When this setting is on, a small dot appears on the corner of that icon for the page in the current tab:

  • Green dot: this page is already in IntelFeed. Click the icon, then "Open", to go to it.
  • Dot in your theme's accent color: the site has an RSS feed, or is a GitHub repository, that you don't follow yet. Click the icon, then "Follow", to add it to your feeds.
  • No dot: neither of the above.

To check, the extension sends the address of each page you open to your server.

Highlight threat intelligence on pages

Underlines names IntelFeed already knows (threat actors, malware, CVEs, CWEs, ATT&CK techniques, tools, vendors, products, countries, sectors, mitigations and indicators) in the color IntelFeed uses for each type. Hover over an underlined name to see its description and a link to its page in IntelFeed. From the same card you can add it to your watchlist, copy its name, or add a country to one of your intel profiles. To find the names, the extension sends the visible text of each page, up to 120,000 characters, to your server.

Show what IntelFeed knows on CVE and ATT&CK pages

On a page about one CVE, such as its page on nvd.nist.gov or cve.org or a vendor's security advisory, a panel in the bottom-right corner of the page shows: the CVSS score, whether it is on the CISA Known Exploited Vulnerabilities (KEV) list, its EPSS score, its exploit status, how many articles in IntelFeed mention it, how many detection rules cover it, and whether it affects a vendor or product in your stack.

On an ATT&CK technique page on attack.mitre.org, the panel shows how many detection rules in IntelFeed are mapped to the technique and the most recent articles that mention it. Close the panel with the × in its corner. The CVE or technique ID in the page's address is sent to your server.

Sites where highlighting is turned off

You turned highlighting off for these sites from the toolbar popup. Remove a site to turn highlighting back on there.

    Right-click menu

    These items are always in the right-click menu:

    • Send page to IntelFeed (right-click anywhere on a page): adds the page to IntelFeed.
    • Send link to IntelFeed (right-click a link): your server downloads the linked page and adds it.
    • Save selection as a detection rule (right-click selected text): opens a window where you check the rule's syntax, save it to IntelFeed's rule library, and convert it to other rule languages.

    Look up selected text in IntelFeed

    Adds "Look up in IntelFeed" to the right-click menu when text is selected. It opens an IntelFeed search for that text in a new tab. Defanged indicators, such as hxxp://example[.]com, are converted back to their normal form before searching.

    Sending

    Tell me what IntelFeed found

    After you send a new page, IntelFeed links its threat actors, malware, CVEs and techniques, and its AI analysis may take a few minutes. When it has finished, a notification says what it found. Clicking the notification opens the article. The extension checks once a minute, for up to 30 minutes.

    When your server can't be reached, a page you send is kept in the browser and sent once the server answers again. The toolbar button shows how many are waiting, and the popup can send them now or discard them. Up to 25 pages are kept. A PDF is not kept: send it again when the server is back.

    Address bar

    To search IntelFeed from the address bar, type if, then a space, then what you are looking for, and press Enter. For example, if APT29 or if CVE-2024-3400. The search opens in the current tab. Hold Alt (Option on a Mac) while pressing Enter to open it in a new tab.

    About

    Version

    New versions are published on the IntelFeed releases page on GitHub. The extension does not update itself. Once a day it checks that page and tells you here and in the toolbar popup when a newer version is available.

    Theme

    The extension uses the theme selected in your IntelFeed account. Sign in to load it.
